behavior spec

Banner leads: Free = empty circle (the not-joined glyph), Focused = live 36pt micro arc, Unlocked = open lock on the warm emergency tint. The Re-focus button is gentle and secondary — re-focusing is invited, never demanded. PermissionHealthRow's off state sits on the orange family (amber posture, §8): it's a fixable health item, not a violation — red would call it a crime.

AllowedAppsRow uses generic glyphs + the policy's labels, not real app icons — honest about the fact that students map policy names to actual apps themselves (S5). 48pt chips, 44pt min touch target respected.
