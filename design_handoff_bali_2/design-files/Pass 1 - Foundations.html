Bali · Pass 1 of 4

Foundations

Rationale. Everything is built to feel like a shared agreement, not enforcement: warm stone neutrals give both platforms paper-like calm instead of clinical blue-gray; a desaturated evergreen carries both the brand and the focused state, so "the product's color" literally means "things are fine." Students get a dark-first world of true warm darks where elevation comes from surface steps and hairlines, not shadows — native, not inverted. The semantic system is rigid on purpose: orange is reserved for emergency and reads earthy and supportive, red exists only for revoked and destructive actions, and every state ships as an icon + label + color triple so the grid survives grayscale and a 6-foot squint. Type does the personality work — Instrument Sans (kept: humanist warmth plus true tabular numerals) for web UI, the system stack on iOS, SF Pro Rounded only at large numeral sizes as the single note of softness, and a mono face strictly for codes.

08 · §3 anti-default self-check

What drifted generic in drafting, and the correction
✓Cream + serif + terracotta: first draft of the neutrals warmed toward cream. Pulled chroma down to near-imperceptible warmth (stone ramp), kept all display type sans, and there is no terracotta — the warm hue in the system is the emergency orange, which is semantic, not decorative.
✓Near-black + acid green: dark mode pairs warm darks (#141312, not #000/#111 blue-black) with a desaturated evergreen that jumps to the soft 300/400 steps — never neon-on-void.
✓Emergency drifted alarm-ward: an early orange-500 read like a hazard vest next to red. Re-anchored the ramp toward amber-brown and specced emergency chips at orange-800-on-orange-100 (light) / 300-on-deep-tint (dark) — warm and calm, clearly distinct from revoked red even in grayscale via the open-lock icon.
✓Gradient meshes / glassmorphism: none. Elevation is two flat systems — warm soft shadows in light, surface-lightness steps + hairlines in dark.
✓Overused faces: no Inter/Roboto. Kept the brief's Instrument Sans suggestion (one-line justification: true tabular numerals plus humanist warmth that matches the paper neutrals). Mono confined to codes; SF Rounded confined to large numerals.
✓Emoji-as-iconography: zero. SF Symbols (iOS) / Lucide (web), one stroke weight.
