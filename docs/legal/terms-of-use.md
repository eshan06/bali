# Bali terms of use — DRAFT for the lawyer

> **Not live. Not legal advice.** A first draft, adapted from General Legal's "Terms of Use"
> template (CC0, https://github.com/General-Legal/legal-templates) for an app and portal
> used by students and teachers, filled in from what Bali does as of 2026-10-24. Every
> fact is filled in; the questions for the lawyer are listed at the end. Sections 1–5 follow the outline the portal's `/terms` page already shows; the
> rest are the template's standard terms, cut down to what fits.

**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

These terms are an agreement between you and Eshan Shah and Jonathan Bani, who run Bali
("Bali", "we", "us"), for using the Bali iPhone app and the Bali portal
(together, "Bali"). By creating an account or using Bali, you agree to them. If you don't
agree, don't use Bali. Our [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) is part of these terms.

## 1. Who Bali is for

- **Students** 13 or older, in a class whose teacher uses Bali.
- **Teachers** at a school that has an agreement with us. A teacher account needs an invite
  code we make for that school.
- **During the Vanderbilt pilot**, accounts are open only to `@vanderbilt.edu` addresses,
  and the app is a test version delivered through Apple's TestFlight.

If your school has an agreement with us, that agreement also applies. Where it and these
terms disagree, the school agreement wins.

## 2. Your account

- Give your real name and keep it current. Your teacher sees it.
- Keep your password to yourself. You're responsible for what happens under your account.
  Tell us right away if you think someone else got in.
- One account per person, on your own iPhone.

## 3. Using Bali in class

- **Tapping in.** When you hold your iPhone to your teacher's Bali block, your apps go quiet
  until the bell, through Apple's Screen Time. Calls, FaceTime, Messages and Emergency SOS
  keep working.
- **Emergency Unlock.** Always available, with or without a connection, and nobody has to
  approve it. Every unlock is recorded, and your teacher sees it, along with the reason if
  you pick one.
- **The bell.** Your apps come back on their own at the end of the session, even if Bali is
  closed or your phone is offline.
- **Screen Time.** You can turn it off for Bali in Settings at any time. Your teacher sees
  when you do during class.
- **Your school's rules come first.** Bali is a tool your teacher chooses. How a class
  handles unlocks or phones is up to your teacher and school, not to us.

### Teachers also agree to

- Use Bali only for the classes they teach, under their school's policies.
- Keep their block and its ID to themselves, and tell us if a block is lost.
- Upload only slides and questions they have the right to share, with nothing in them that
  breaks the law or their school's rules.
- Treat what Bali shows about students as school records, under their school's privacy rules.

### Nobody may

- Try to get around Bali's records, for example by faking a tap, a block or a check-in, or
  by changing the app.
- Try to see another person's information, or get into an account or class that isn't theirs.
- Overload, scan or attack Bali's servers, or copy Bali to build a competing product.
- Use Bali to harass anyone, or to break the law.

## 4. Bali is not a safety device

Bali never blocks emergency calls, and Emergency Unlock always works. Still, don't rely on
Bali in an emergency. If your phone has no signal, is off or is broken, Bali can't help.
Bali also can't promise that every app will be quieted, since that depends on Apple's
Screen Time.

## 5. Leaving Bali

- **Leave a class** in the app any time a session isn't running.
- **Delete your account** in the app (Me → Delete account). Our privacy policy, section 7,
  says what that removes and what stays as counts that name no one. A teacher with a class
  or block deletes theirs through their school.
- **We may suspend or close an account** that breaks these terms or puts others at risk. If
  we do, we'll say why unless the law stops us, and Emergency Unlock keeps working on that
  phone until the bell.
- **If Bali shuts down**, we'll tell schools in advance and handle their records as their
  agreements say.

## 6. Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters, we'll say
so in the app and on the portal before it applies. If you keep using Bali after that, you
accept the new terms. If you don't accept them, delete your account.

## 7. Changes to Bali

Bali is new and still changing, and the pilot version may have bugs. We may change, pause
or stop features. We'll avoid doing it during class hours when we can.

## 8. Ownership and feedback

We own Bali's software, name and design. Teachers keep their own slides and questions, and
give us permission to store and show them to their class only to run Bali. If you send us
ideas, we may use them freely, without owing you anything.

## Notes for the lawyer

1. **Minors.** Students may be 13–17 once K-12 starts. Can a minor accept these terms, or do
   we need a parent's or the school's consent instead? The Vanderbilt pilot is adults only.
2. **Arbitration** was left out on purpose (section 11): forcing students into arbitration
   can hurt a school's trust, and some school agreements forbid it. The template's version
   (binding individual arbitration, class-action and jury-trial waivers) can be added back.
   Tennessee law and Davidson County courts were picked because the pilot is at Vanderbilt.
3. **The liability cap and indemnity.** Is a cap enforceable against students? Indemnity from
   students was cut. The template's section 4 makes users pay our legal
   costs, which seems wrong to ask of students. Confirm, or add it back for teachers only.
4. **Apple's Licensed Application terms** (the App Store's standard EULA) may need to be
   referenced, or replaced with our own Apple-compliant terms.
5. **The operators** are two individuals. An LLC changes the parties and the liability picture.
6. **The Pilot agreement** with the professor or Vanderbilt is a separate document (PLAN's
   go-live list). It isn't drafted here, since neither app shows it.
