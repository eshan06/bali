## Notes for the lawyer

1. **Minors.** Students may be 13–17 once K-12 starts. Can a minor accept these terms, or do
   we need a parent's or the school's consent instead? The Vanderbilt pilot is adults only.
2. **Arbitration** was left out on purpose (section 11): forcing students into arbitration
   can hurt a school's trust, and some school agreements forbid it. The template's version
   (binding individual arbitration, class-action and jury-trial waivers) can be added back.
   Tennessee law and Davidson County courts were picked because the pilot is at Vanderbilt.
3. **The liability cap and indemnity.** Is a cap enforceable against students? Indemnity from
   students was cut. The template's section 4 makes users pay our legal
   costs, which seems wrong to ask of students. Confirm, or add it back for teachers only.
4. **Apple's Licensed Application terms** (the App Store's standard EULA) may need to be
   referenced, or replaced with our own Apple-compliant terms.
5. **The operators** are two individuals. An LLC changes the parties and the liability picture.
6. **The Pilot agreement** with the professor or Vanderbilt is a separate document (PLAN's
   go-live list). It isn't drafted here, since neither app shows it.
