How a class starts

What happens on the teacher's screen, on the Bali block and on a student's phone, in order.

Before the first class

  • A phone for each studentWith the Bali app: an iPhone on iOS 17 or later, or an Android phone.
  • A browser for the teacherAny computer works. The portal runs in the browser, with nothing to install.
  • A Bali blockOne per classroom. Students tap their phones on it.
  • A school email addressThe teacher signs in to the portal with it.

The first class, step by step

  1. 1Start class from any browser.

    The teacher signs in to the portal and starts the session. The live grid opens with a tile for every student in the class.

  2. 2New students join once.

    A student new to the class joins once with the class code. After that, a tap is all it takes.

  3. 3Students tap in.

    Each student holds their phone to the teacher's Bali block. Their apps lock on the spot, even without Wi-Fi, and their tile turns to Focused.

  4. 4Teach, and watch the room.

    The grid shows who is focused, who has unlocked and who is not here yet. Slides and questions reach every phone and the student web.

  5. 5The bell rings.

    When class ends, the apps open on their own and the teacher gets a recap, every unlock included.

  6. Planning the first class?

    We can help set up the class, the roster and the Bali block before day one.

    Talk to us

Emergency Unlock is always there.

A student holds for one second and their apps open. Saying why is optional. The unlock shows on the teacher's grid and in the recap, and calls, texts and emergency calls never lock.
