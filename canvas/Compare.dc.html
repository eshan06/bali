How schools keep phones out of class.

The common ways, side by side: how each works, who controls it, what it costs and what schools have reported.

The ways compared

WayHow it worksWho controls itReported costWhat schools report
BaliStudents tap their phone on the Bali block when class starts, and their apps lock until the bell. Calls, texts and Emergency Unlock always work.The teacher starts class. A student can always unlock, and every unlock is recorded.Talk to usNew. Talk to us about a pilot.
Lockable pouchesStudents seal their phone in a pouch they carry all day. Staff unlock it at dismissal with a magnetic base.School staffAbout $25 to $30 per studentTIME and Yahoo News, 2024Delaware's 2026 state pilot: more than 80% of teachers and staff backed the pouches; about 70% of students didn't want them kept.
Lockers or cabinetsPhones go into wall lockers or locked cabinets for the day.The schoolFrom $40,700 (Olathe, KS) to about $1.6 million (DeKalb County, GA)District purchases, 2025 to 2026No outcome studies specific to lockers found.
Pocket caddiesPhones go into numbered pockets at the start of each class.Each teacherLow-cost classroom suppliesNot verifiedFewer distractions in class, but phones can be lost or damaged, and enforcement falls on teachers (Wisconsin DPI).
Hand-in or basketsStaff collect phones at the start of the day or of each class.StaffNo equipment; staff time insteadCommon in the Netherlands since the 2024 national rule.
"Off and away"A rule only: phones off and in backpacks.The student, under the ruleFreeEven under no-use rules, half of students still check a phone in class every day (RAND, 2026).
Apple Screen TimeA parent sets schedules and limits on a child's iPhone through Family Sharing.A parent, or the studentFreeBuilt for families. It doesn't give a school control of a student's own iPhone.
Apple ClassroomA teacher app that can open and lock apps on student iPads and Macs.School IT or the teacherFree; managed classes need device managementApple lists iPad and Mac as student devices, not iPhone.
Google Family LinkA parent schedules "School time" on a child's Android phone: limited apps, with calls and texts allowed.A parentFreeNeeds a supervised Google Account.
Android Focus modeThe phone's owner pauses chosen apps, on a schedule if they like.The studentFreeThe student can turn it off at any time.

Costs vary by school and supplier; the figures above are the ones reported in the sources, with their dates. Checked November 6, 2026.
