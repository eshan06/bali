Emergency Unlock, explained.

A student can always get to their phone, and every unlock is recorded.

How it works

  1. 1Hold for one second.

    On the Focus screen, the student holds the unlock button for one second, and their apps open right away.

  2. 2Say why, if they want to.

    Bali offers three reasons: Bathroom, Nurse or Other. Picking one is optional, and the student can change it while they are unlocked.

  3. 3The teacher sees it.

    The unlock shows on the teacher's grid right away and in the recap after the bell, with the reason if the student gave one.

After an unlock

Coming back

When the student is ready, they return to focus and their tile turns back to Focused. The recap shows when they came back.

Always on

Teachers can't switch Emergency Unlock off, and every unlock stays in the class's record. Calls, texts and emergency calls keep working all class, locked or not.
