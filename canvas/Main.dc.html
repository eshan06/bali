Questions

Something we didn't cover? Talk to us.

What happens in an emergency?

Students can unlock any time by holding for one second, and their apps open right away. Calls, FaceTime, Messages and Emergency SOS never lock. Every unlock shows on the teacher's grid and in the recap.

Does it work without Wi-Fi?

Yes. A tap locks the apps right away with no signal, and the phone tells the teacher's grid once it's back online.

Can a student turn Bali off?

They can switch off Bali's Screen Time access in Settings. If they do, the teacher's grid shows it.

What does a class need to use Bali?

Each student needs a phone with the Bali app: an iPhone on iOS 17 or later, or an Android phone. The teacher needs any web browser, a Bali block in the classroom and a school email address to sign in.
