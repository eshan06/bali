A note for families.

A short letter a teacher can send home before the first class with Bali. The highlighted parts are the ones to fill in.

Dear families,

This term, the class uses Bali during class time. When class starts, each student taps their phone on the Bali block in the classroom, and their apps lock until the bell. Students keep their phones the whole time.

Calls, texts and emergency calls always work. A student can open their apps at any time with Emergency Unlock: they hold one button for one second, and saying why is optional. The teacher sees that the unlock happened, and when.

Bali never sees a student's screen, messages, location or apps. During class, the teacher sees who is focused, when each student taps in or unlocks, and the reason if they share one. Answers to class questions only count toward the class's totals.

When the bell rings, every app comes back on its own.

Families with questions can reply to this note.

What families often ask

Can a student reach home during class?

Yes. Calls and texts always work, and Emergency Unlock opens every app in one second.

Does Bali see a student's location?

No. Bali never sees a student's location, screen, messages or apps.

What does the teacher see?
